The most important bit

Why we give windows, not exact dates

Every installation is a little different. If the build before yours needs extra groundwork, decking or unexpected site work, our team can't leave that customer halfway through to arrive at the next job.

The same courtesy would be extended to you, should your installation need extra time.

A realistic window means we can finish every build to the standard it deserves, yours included.

Your journey

What happens next

Step 1

Order confirmed

Your order is in our production system and the team starts coordinating everything your build needs.

Step 2

Materials ordered

Nothing comes off a shelf. Your materials are ordered just for your build, which may include Thermowood or Nordic Spruce, bespoke windows, custom timber rings, a Harvia heater, lighting, roofing, glass and electrical components.

Step 3

Production queue

Once everything arrives, your build moves through our Caerphilly workshop. Every project is bespoke, so build times vary with the specification.

Step 4

Production update

At around 6 weeks we aim to have a clearer picture of progress, your completion window and installation planning.

You don't need to wait for us. Message us on WhatsApp any time and we'll tell you where your build is.

Step 5

Installation scheduling

As production nears completion, we arrange delivery or installation with you. This is normally 2 to 3 weeks after your production update, depending on location, installation type and workload.

If we're building on site

Most builds are done in one visit

Most of the time our team arrives with everything needed and completes the build in one go. Before then, we'll check that everything is workable at your property, including access for the team and materials.

Occasionally, depending on where you sit on our installation route, the team may do things a little differently:

Everything on the van

We arrive with all materials ready to begin straight away.

Delivered ahead

Materials arrive a day or two before the team does.

Groundwork first

Groundwork or decking starts before the main structure.

It lets our carpenters make the most of their time on site rather than travelling back and forth to Wales.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to what most people ask

01

Can I make changes to my order?

Minor changes may be possible before production begins. Once materials are ordered or manufacturing has started, changes may not be possible or may add cost.

02

How do I get an update?

Message us on WhatsApp whenever you like and the team will tell you where your build is.

03

Can I get weekly updates?

We don't send routine weekly updates because every build moves at its own pace. We'll be in touch at the key milestones, and you can message us on WhatsApp any time in between.

04

Can my installation window change?

Occasionally, yes. Weather, the installation before yours or supplier lead times can affect scheduling. We'll keep you informed of any significant change.

05

Can I add items to my order?

Yes. Everything we build is made in the same style and materials, so it all matches. Ask as early as you can and we can aim to bring everything at once, saving you on delivery.
