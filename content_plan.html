The steam room

Built for steam

A steam room is a wet room, so it is built differently from the sauna beside it. Here is what we build, and what your side provides before we install.

What we build
  • Curved tiled ceiling, so condensation runs down the walls and never drips on you
  • Fully tiled walls, floor and bench. No timber inside the steam room
  • Toughened glass door with stainless steel hinges and bar handle
  • Harvia 4.5 kW steam generator with WiFi control, sized to the room
What your side provides before installation
  • A drain connection at the cabin for the steam room floor
  • A cold water feed to the generator
  • A 230V electrical supply on a 25A fuse, connected by your qualified electrician
  • A dry, ventilated service space for the generator within 6m of the steam room
