Who should be ready when

The 10 week model: every paid deposit is plotted from the day it landed to ten weeks later, when the build should be ready. Week 6 is the production update call, week 9 the pre install check.

What it's for: see at a glance which installs are coming up, so filming is planned before the build leaves the workshop, and the review ask goes out once it is in.

Where the rows come from: the builds tab of the BSUK content plan sheet. The Deposit Paid workflow adds a row the moment a deposit lands. Marta can type a real ETA into the sheet and it shows here as a honey marker.
