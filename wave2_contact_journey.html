🗺️ Wave 2 Contact Journey ✨

every step a Wave 2 lead takes, workflow by workflow 🧭

notes autosave on every keystroke · last saved: not yet

A new Facebook lead is captured, tagged, handed to AMP and greeted within a minute, whatever the hour.

1
📋 Facebook lead form submitted
The customer completes the Meta lead form and enters the system.
2
🏷️ Tagged as a Wave 2 lead
The tag wave 2 facebook/meta lead is applied. This tag gates everything downstream.
3
📊 Moved to New Lead stage
The opportunity is created at the top of the pipeline.
4
☎️ Assigned to AMP
The external call team owns the contact from this point.
5
💬 Instant greeting WhatsApp
Fires the moment the form arrives, day or night, while the enquiry is still warm.
6
⏰ Hold until open hours
If the WhatsApp delivered: a short hold so the rest lands between 10:30am and 5pm on weekdays, then the branded greeting email and the first AMP call is bridged.
7
📲 Fallback path
If the WhatsApp did not deliver: the same greeting goes by SMS, then the email, the AMP call, and a day later the Deal of the Month email.
📝 My notes on 1️⃣ W1

Three sibling workflows listen to every call and route the lead by outcome. Each acts only on Wave 2 leads and knows whether the lead has already been reached, so nobody gets pulled backwards.

1
📗 W2(a) call connected
First connected call only: the lead moves to contacted by office and is stamped with the reached tag every other workflow reads.
2
📙 W2(b) call not answered
A missed call moves the lead to Not Reached and records the outcome. Two days later the team gets a follow up email and AMP gets a call-again task. If the lead is reached in the meantime, it ends early and both are skipped.
3
📘 W2(c) lead rings us back
An inbound call that connects counts as the first conversation: moved to Contacted, stamped as reached, and pulled out of the no-answer net.
📝 My notes on 2️⃣ W2

After every AMP conversation the call is transcribed and classified by AI as Confirmed, Thinking, No or Unclear, and the outcome routes what happens next. Leads with a call already booked are left alone.

1
🔥 Warm (Confirmed or Thinking)
Moved to reached and warm, a 5 minute pause, then the was-great-speaking-with-you WhatsApp with the workshop video and SMS fallback, followed by the Get To Know Us email.
2
🧊 Cold (No)
Moved to reached but cold, then the cross sell WhatsApp: we do more than just barrel saunas. SMS fallback plus a hand-written style cross sell email.
3
❓ Unclear
Tagged for the follow up net, which picks the lead up 48 hours later if nothing resolves.
📝 My notes on 3️⃣ W3

Warm leads get a gentle, human paced sequence from Marta. Cold leads who reply get promoted straight back into it.

1
🌱 Warm nurture
3 days after going warm: how's it going? 4 days later: can I help with anything? 3 days more: sorry, forgot to mention, with the Book a Call Back link. 7 quiet days later the lead is tagged as gone quiet and moves to the 8 week list. Full WhatsApp, SMS and email fallbacks, and booking a call pulls the lead out instantly.
2
🔄 Cold revival
Any reply from a cold lead removes the cold tag, promotes them to warm, and they flow into nurture like any other warm lead.
📝 My notes on 4️⃣ W4

When the first AMP call goes unanswered the system retries once, then chases politely, and knows exactly when to stop.

1
☎️ Automatic retry next day
The day after the miss, at lunchtime, a fresh AMP call is bridged. First time through only.
2
🛡️ Did anything land?
A day later: if the retry connected, or the lead replied, booked or paid, the chase ends silently.
3
💬 The chase
Otherwise: we tried giving you a ring, with the Book a call back link; 2 days later are you still looking; 3 days later a last chance. Every message mirrored on SMS and email, and a reply pulls the lead out instantly.
4
📊 Chase exhausted
Silent leads are marked exhausted and move to Cold. Leads who received every message also carry the revival tag, so a reply weeks later still promotes them back to warm.
📝 My notes on 5️⃣ W5

A confirmed booking on the Book a Call Back calendar switches the lead from being chased to being prepared for the closer.

1
🏷️ Booking tagged
The wave 2 call booked tag is applied and every other workflow stands down.
2
🎣 Pulled out of nurture
Removed from warm nurture and the no answer chase, stage moves to call booked with closer.
3
💬 Confirmation from Marta
A WhatsApp confirms the time with a reschedule link, with SMS and email fallbacks.
4
🔔 Team warned about quiet leads
The confirmation email is watched for 2 days: leads who open but don't reschedule, and leads who never engage at all, both trigger a heads up so the closer knows who might not show.
📝 My notes on 6️⃣ W6

The closer call is classified the same way as the first, and only leads with a booked call are routed here.

1
✅ Confirmed
The deposit link email goes out.
2
🤔 Thinking
2 quiet days, then have you had a chance to think it over, with the lead-times nudge, on every channel. If the lead stays quiet the team is alerted, immediately when no channel delivers.
3
🚫 No
The lead is tagged cold with 7 days of grace. Any sign of life, a reply, a deposit, an estimate, a booking, being away or saving for later, flags them for human follow up with a team alert. Everyone else moves to the 8 week list.
4
❓ Unclear
The team is alerted directly, a person handles the nuance rather than automation.
📝 My notes on 7️⃣ W7

Leads parked on the marketing list rest, then get two well spaced touches.

1
📧 8 weeks: where are you at with your build?
A personal check-in that lands with the team when they reply.
2
📧 12 weeks: we build more than saunas
The full range email: hot tubs, ice baths, glamping pods, mobile saunas, grill cabins.
3
🏁 Instant exit
Booking a call or paying a deposit at any point ends the sequence immediately and quietly.
📝 My notes on 8️⃣ W8

Warm leads who go quiet are parked where the team can see them, and a single reply brings them back.

1
🚪 Entry
7 quiet days after going warm, and only if no conversation is running, the lead is tagged, moved to warm pending, and the team is alerted.
2
🔄 Exit on reply
Any reply moves them straight back to reached and warm and removes the pending tag.
📝 My notes on 9️⃣ W9

Three workflows cover the showroom visit end to end. The showroom calendar sends its own confirmation and reminder emails, so these stay out of the customer's inbox.

1
📅 W10(a) visit booked
A confirmed booking on the Showroom Bookings calendar tags the lead and pulls them out of nurture and the chase.
2
👣 W10(b) visited
When the appointment is marked as showed, the lead is tagged visited showroom and the stage moves to match.
3
🔔 W10(c) outcome
After the appointment ends: no-shows trigger a team alert, visitors end quiet.
📝 My notes on 🔟 W10

An unclear outcome from either router starts a 48 hour clock.

1
⏳ 48 hours of grace
If anything resolves in that window, a reply, a booking, a payment, a clean outcome, the net lets go silently.
2
🔔 Unresolved
Otherwise the team is alerted: unclear call, still unresolved, human needed.
📝 My notes on 1️⃣1️⃣ W11

Any completed call with a Wave 2 lead means a real conversation happened, so the automation steps back.

1
🎣 Pulled from the sequences
The lead is removed from warm nurture and the no answer chase.
2
🔥 Cold leads warm up
A cold lead who has an actual conversation is promoted back to reached and warm.
📝 My notes on 1️⃣2️⃣ W12

The estimate stage starts a short, polite countdown to the deposit.

1
📧 Deposit link email
The estimate is followed by the securing-your-build email with the deposit link, and the team is notified.
2
💬 3 days: friendly check-in
Unpaid leads get a light WhatsApp and a personal style email: just checking in, reply if anything has changed.
3
🔔 2 more days: team alert
Still unpaid and quiet, a person takes over.
4
🏁 Payment ends everything
A successful £500 deposit at any moment ends the chase instantly.
📝 My notes on 1️⃣3️⃣ W13

The moment the £500 deposit clears, on any pipeline, the celebration and the handover begin.

1
🏷️ Tagged and moved
The deposit paid tag fires every guard in the system, and the stage moves to deposit paid.
2
💬 Just a quick thanks
A short WhatsApp from Marta confirms it landed and points to the email.
3
📧 Branded thank you email
Deposit received, the journey circles showing deposit paid as the active stage, and what happens next.
4
🥳 Team celebration alert
The good-news internal email goes to the assigned user and Marta: contact details, £500 received, next steps, open in GHL.
