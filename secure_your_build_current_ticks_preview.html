What your deposit does

  • The £500 deposit is deducted in full from your final invoice. It is not an extra charge.
  • It reserves your place in the queue for 72 hours while the final details of your sauna are confirmed.
  • No materials are ordered and no production begins until your invoice is paid.
  • Once your specification is agreed, our admin team issues your official invoice.
  • Not ready yet? Your deposit still opens up priority support, renders, more time on your proposal and a site visit.

72 hours

You do not lose your deposit.

If the invoice is not settled within 72 hours your place in the queue may be released. Your deposit simply allows us to focus our time. The £500 deposit is non-refundable.

What happens next

🤝

Deposit received

Your £500 commitment deposit is received and your place in the queue is reserved for 72 hours.

📝

Specification confirmed

A member of our team contacts you to confirm your final specification and answer any remaining questions.

🧾

Invoice issued

Our admin team issues your official invoice based on the agreed specification.

🌲

Order starts

Your order officially starts once that invoice is paid, and we begin ordering the materials for your build.

💷

Balance options

You can hold back the VAT plus £1,000 until your build is complete. Your invoice updates to show as partially paid.

🔨

Into production

Your project is scheduled into production and we send your job intake form to capture any final project information.

📞

Your point of contact

Your order moves to the next department. Marta or Millie will be your point of contact from here through to handover.
