How to use it for a new job

Everything is in metres. x runs east from the west edge of the site, z runs south from the north edge. The plan shows north at the top.

rot is the way the item faces, in degrees clockwise from south: 0 south, 90 west, 180 north, 270 east. For a fence it is the direction it runs.

base lifts an item off the ground, for example base:0.6 to sit it on 600 high decking.

Item types: barrel_sauna, cube_sauna, hot_tub_round, hot_tub_square, ice_bath, double_dipper, shepherds_hut, glamping_pod, mobile_sauna, outdoor_shower, building, decking, steps, pergola, fence, path, gravel, lawn, tree, person.

Default product sizes are working figures. Put the real size on each line for the job.
