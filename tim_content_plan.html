PriorityTalking piece

Hi, it's Tim from Barrel Sauna UK. Thank you for paying your deposit. You're now in our order queue.

Next, we'll send you your invoice for the full build. Please go through it carefully. If anything you want is missing, tell us now, because we build it exactly as it is on your invoice.

Sent with the invoice

What your ETA means

PriorityTalking piece

Why we give a time window, not a fixed date, and why not to book guests yet.

Draft script

Your build comes with an ETA, an estimated time. It's usually around eight to ten weeks.

It's an estimate because things outside our hands can move it. A storm can shut sites across the country, and builds ahead of yours can run over. That's why we give you a window, not a fixed date.

So please don't take bookings or plan guests around a date yet. If you were building an Airbnb, you wouldn't take guests until it was finished. Once your build is done and signed off, that's the time to start.

Ringing us for a date won't make it come sooner, but we will keep you updated.

Sent around week 5

Sent before install

We're only human, and we always put it right

PriorityTalking piece

Sets expectations on snags, and asks for patience and respect both ways.

Draft script

One more thing. We're a team that has built hundreds of saunas, and this process is how we work for good reason. Trust the process.

We're only human. If anything needs fixing, we won't stop until it's fully sorted. If a light comes out of the box with a broken bulb, we'll post you a new one. We will never try not to give you what you've paid for.

Be patient with us and we'll be respectful with you. We finish with everything perfect.
