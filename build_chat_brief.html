Heater options

Choose your heat

Every sauna is built bespoke. Four ways to heat it, and one of them will suit how you live.

🔌
Electric switch
The simplest to live with. Turn it on, wait, get in.
Best for
A sauna near the house with power close by.
Think about
An electrician runs a dedicated supply out to the sauna.
📱
Electric WiFi
Switch it on from your phone, so it's hot when you get home. Our top pick.
Best for
Busy weeks, when you want it ready the moment you walk in.
Think about
The same electrics as the switch version, plus WiFi that reaches the garden. Runs on the Harvia app.
🔥
Wood burning
The classic sauna experience. Real fire, no electrics needed.
Best for
Off-grid spots, or anyone who loves the ritual of lighting the stove.
Think about
Internal feed loads from inside the sauna. External feed loads from outside, so no smoke or mess in the sauna.
♨️
Hybrid
Wood burning and electric in one sauna.
Best for
Wood at the weekend, a quick electric session midweek.
Think about
The most flexible of the four, and the one worth talking through with James.
James will askIs there power nearby, or is wood the plan?
